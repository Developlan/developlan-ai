# Developlan AI
## Product Artefacts

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document describes the major user-facing artefacts produced by Developlan AI.

These artefacts represent the product outputs experienced by users. They are not engineering components.

---

# 1. Opportunity Brief

Purpose:
Rapid factual briefing for Business Development.

Audience:
Business Development Manager.

Typical reading time:
2 minutes.

Primary contents:

- Opportunity Snapshot
- Client
- Scope
- Dates
- Geography
- Submission Requirements
- Qualifications
- Experience
- Supporting Documents
- Investigation Confidence

---

# 2. Opportunity Intelligence Package

Purpose:
Deep understanding of the opportunity.

Audience:

- Bid Manager
- Technical Lead
- Management

Typical reading time:
10-15 minutes.

Primary contents:

- Executive Summary
- Investigation Summary
- Evidence Summary
- Opportunity Characteristics
- Risks & Unknowns
- Supporting Evidence
- Investigation Metadata
