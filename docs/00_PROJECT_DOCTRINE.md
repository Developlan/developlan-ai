# Developlan AI
## Project Doctrine

Version: 1.0
Status: Standing Principles

---

# Purpose

Developlan AI is an Intelligence Platform for Business Development.

The objective is not to automate business development tasks.

The objective is to create a capability that discovers, understands, evaluates and develops opportunities more intelligently than conventional search tools, portals, or AI search agents.

The platform should function as an experienced Business Development strategist rather than a document processor.

---

# Intelligence Doctrine

Every capability within the platform should strengthen the following progression:

Capability
↓

Potentiality
↓

Intelligent Opportunity
↓

Proposition

This sequence is the core doctrine of the platform.

---

# Intelligence First

The platform should always prefer intelligent reasoning over deterministic processing.

AI should be used to:

- investigate
- interpret
- infer
- compare
- challenge
- propose

rather than simply:

- classify
- extract
- store
- filter

Extraction supports intelligence.

Extraction is never the objective.

---

# Capability Before Services

The platform should understand what an organisation is capable of achieving.

Not merely what services it currently markets.

The system should recognise adjacent capabilities, emerging strengths, transferable experience, geographic expansion opportunities and strategic partnerships.

Business development should be driven by organisational potential rather than historical service descriptions.

---

# Opportunity Before Compliance

The objective is not to determine whether an organisation satisfies a Terms of Reference.

The objective is to determine whether an organisation could successfully pursue an opportunity.

This includes:

- capability stretch
- consortium options
- partner identification
- technical adaptation
- geographic expansion
- proposition redesign

---

# AI as Investigator

AI should investigate.

Not simply summarise.

Every stage should encourage reasoning.

Examples include:

- identifying hidden opportunities
- investigating donor behaviour
- exploring linked opportunities
- identifying strategic patterns
- recommending adjacent markets

---

# Prototype Philosophy

Prototype before platform.

Every prototype component should demonstrate genuine intelligence.

Avoid premature optimisation.

Avoid unnecessary architecture.

Avoid engineering for hypothetical future requirements.

Build working intelligence first.

---

# Commercial Principle

The commercial value of Developlan AI comes from the quality of its intelligence.

Do not sacrifice intelligence in order to minimise token costs, simplify engineering or reduce computational effort.

A more intelligent recommendation has substantially greater commercial value than a cheaper computation.

---

# Engineering Principle

Every new component should answer one question:

"Does this materially improve the intelligence of the platform?"

If the answer is no,

it probably should not be built.

---

# Standing Rule

Never drift towards building another opportunity database.

Always build a better Business Development intelligence capability.
