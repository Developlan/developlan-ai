# Developlan AI
## Product Vision

Version: 1.0

---

# Product Definition

Developlan AI is an AI-native Business Development Intelligence Platform.

It continuously identifies, investigates, evaluates and develops funding, partnership and commercial opportunities based on an organisation's capabilities and strategic potential.

Rather than helping organisations search for opportunities, Developlan AI helps organisations discover opportunities they should pursue.

---

# Vision

To become the most intelligent Business Development capability available to development organisations, humanitarian agencies, NGOs, Aboriginal organisations and mission-driven enterprises.

The platform should think like an experienced Business Development Director.

---

# The Problem

Most organisations currently rely on:

- donor portals
- email alerts
- keyword searches
- procurement notices
- manual opportunity reviews
- generic AI search agents

These approaches are reactive.

They identify published opportunities but provide very little strategic intelligence.

---

# Our Difference

Developlan AI asks different questions.

Instead of:

"What opportunities exist?"

it asks:

"What opportunities should this organisation pursue?"

Instead of:

"What services does this organisation deliver?"

it asks:

"What is this organisation capable of delivering?"

Instead of:

"Does this opportunity match?"

it asks:

"Could this organisation successfully win and deliver this opportunity?"

---

---

# Product Functions

The platform should:

• understand organisational capability

• continuously monitor opportunity ecosystems

• investigate opportunities using AI

• identify strategic fit

• identify capability stretch

• recommend partnerships and consortiums

• assess competitive positioning

• generate propositions

• support proposal development

---

# Sources of Intelligence

The platform should operate across multiple intelligence sources, including:

- donor portals
- procurement portals
- funding databases
- email alerts
- RSS feeds
- websites
- APIs
- document repositories
- social media (where valuable)
- news and announcements

The platform should integrate these sources into a single intelligence process.

---

# Commercial Principle

Clients are purchasing intelligence.

Not software.

Not document extraction.

Not another opportunity database.

Every capability should increase the quality of business development decisions.

---

# Success Measure

The platform succeeds when it identifies opportunities that a client would not otherwise have pursued and helps convert those opportunities into successful proposals.
